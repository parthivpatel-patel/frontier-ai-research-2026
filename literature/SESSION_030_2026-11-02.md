## Methodological contribution and prior-art audit

This session closes an important reproducibility gap: the set of allowed candidate operators was declared before measuring which unknown words occur in the external predictions. It avoids post-hoc vocabulary selection and prevents the new diagnostic from silently converting unsupported cases into claimed model errors. This is **good experimental practice, not an original algorithm or a novelty finding**.

[Spot's documented LTL semantics](https://spot.lre.epita.fr/concepts.html) establish that release (R/V), weak until (W), and strong release (M) are standard temporal operators; [Spot's LTL examples](https://spot.lre.epita.fr/ltldo.html) include xor and equivalence. If future work extends the parser, precedence and infinite-trace semantics must be frozen and independently cross-checked. No parser extension occurred in this session.

Strong nearby research remains [VLTL-Bench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.00877), [ConformalNL2LTL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.21022), [SCP-NL2TL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05439), [NeuroNL2LTL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.22874) and [VerusEval](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26464). The possible new hypothesis — calibrated uncertainty in independently validated physical predicates/dynamics can reduce unsafe acceptance at matched coverage — remains **unproven**.
