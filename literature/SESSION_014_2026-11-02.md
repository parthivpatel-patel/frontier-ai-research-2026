All 1,806 question IDs match the identifiers in **each** inspected model-output file after string normalization. This is a concrete feasibility milestone for source-aligned, recorded-model analysis. It does **not** establish correct labels, true model identities, matching prompt semantics, model accuracy, or independently verified generation provenance.

The question schema has an `ANSWER` field (list in 1,704 rows and string in 102), and `CALCULUS`, `TYPE`, `QUESTION`, and `ID` fields. Model files contain `answer`, `id`, `repeat`, `request`, `response`, `provider`, `model`, and `timestamp` fields. Upstream's `request` and `response` structures differ between providers; a uniform parser must not be assumed.

**Crucial task distinction:** QSTRBench evaluates qualitative spatial/temporal relation reasoning, not NL-to-LTL translation or physical robot dynamics. It can be a separately labeled secondary model-output audit, but **cannot** be counted as evidence that our main physics-grounded translation hypothesis works.

## Literature novelty / falsification update

1. Cohn and Blackwell (2026), https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.18380 , already benchmark multiple qualitative spatial/temporal calculi and release model outputs. We cannot claim a new general QSTR benchmark.
2. Li, Hogg and Cohn (2024), https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.15064 , already study realistic spatial reasoning and logic-based checking of multiple plausible solutions. We cannot claim that physically grounded spatial ambiguity checking is new.
3. Bisharat et al. (2026), *TLA+-Bench*, https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.23425 , already show that verification choices strongly change measured NL-to-formal correctness.
4. English et al. (2025), *VLTL-Bench*, https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.00877 , already investigate trace-grounded temporal-logic translation.
5. Classical vacuity results (Beer et al., 2001; Kupferman and Vardi, 2003) preclude presenting missing counterexamples under restrictive dynamics as a new theoretical observation.

**Scientific decision:** The available evidence supports a reproducible **feasibility audit**, not a novel methods paper. We should not reinterpret a dataset join, exact toy proof, or test pass as a new scientific discovery.
