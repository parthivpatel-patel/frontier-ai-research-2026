### Interpretation and independence

The same four original input documents were processed five times each. The 98 expression records are **not 98 independent human instructions**, and the 20 files are **not 20 independent sources**. Different extraction counts mean a positional comparison of formulas across runs is invalid without independently established alignment. Even identical counts would not prove semantic correspondence. No generated formula, original source text or third-party JSON was redistributed; only aggregate structural statistics are published.

The upstream repository attributes its pipeline to a GPT-4 configuration, but the exact historical model, prompt execution and output provenance are not independently authenticated. The source documents and generated outputs have unresolved file-specific redistribution rights. **We did not measure semantic correctness, hallucination, safety, physics grounding, or model accuracy.** No inference or training occurred.

## 2. Stronger novelty exclusions from new literature audit

The following closely related methods predate the present project:

1. **Godefroid (2013), *May/Must Abstraction-Based Software Model Checking For Sound Verification and Falsification***: three-valued abstractions and generalized model checking explicitly reason over incomplete system models. https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/maymust-abstraction-based-software-model-checking-for-sound-verification-and-falsification/ . This is direct prior art against claiming that evaluating uncertain or partial physical transition systems is novel.
2. **Anevlavis, Philippe, Neider and Tabuada (2022), *Being Correct Is Not Enough: Efficient Verification Using Robust Linear Temporal Logic***: formal robustness under environment-assumption violations. https://doi.org/10.1145/3491216 . This is prior art against generic claims about robustness to physical assumptions.
3. **Rabiei et al., *LTLCodeGen* (IROS 2025)**: LLM-to-LTL translation coupled to a semantic map and robot motion planning, with simulation and real-world evaluations. https://doi.org/10.1109/IROS60139.2025.11246351 .
4. **Grigorev, Kovalev and Panov, *VerifyLLM* (IROS 2025)**: LLM conversion of natural language to LTL followed by plan checking. https://doi.org/10.1109/IROS60139.2025.11246320 .
5. **Hou et al. (August 28, 2026), *Linear Temporal Logic Translation via Human-Inspired Self-Constrained Reasoning for Robot Task Specification***: explicitly embeds domain constraints in NL-to-LTL reasoning. https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28435 .

Combined with prior audits of VLTL-Bench, VERIFY, SCP-NL2TL, LTLGUARD and repeated NL-to-LTL evaluation, this **further weakens broad novelty claims** about physical constraints, verification, robustness, uncertainty, semantic consistency, or repeated generations. None of these papers alone establishes that every narrower proposed experiment is redundant; a method-level comparison remains necessary.

## 3. Next falsifiable research gate

The new structural audit establishes that output alignment and semantic ground truth **cannot be presumed**. Before using these predictions for model evaluation, independently determine (a) whether individual requirements can be identified without violating third-party rights, (b) whether at least 30 genuinely independent source requirements with human-intended semantics can be assembled and adjudicated, (c) whether formulas can be mapped to those requirements without positional matching, and (d) whether matched baselines already explain any observed failure.

**Candidate question, not a novelty claim:** Given independent semantic annotations and multiple plausible transition systems, can an explicitly assumption-aware *selective diagnostic* improve over may/must model checking, VLTL-Bench grounded verification, SCP-NL2TL selective translation and ordinary clarification at matched false-positive rates? If not, report the null and pivot to a reproducibility/limitations paper. Do not present the four-document census as an AI reliability benchmark.
