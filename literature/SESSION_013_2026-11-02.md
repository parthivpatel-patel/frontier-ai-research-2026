# Session 013 — Why restricting physics can hide specification errors

Date: 2026-11-02 (US Eastern). **Novelty gate: NOT PASSED. Publication claim: NOT READY.**

## Research finding (toy systems, not AI performance)

The Session 012 exact checker established seven directional separating witnesses on the flexible actuator across eight directional comparisons, but only one on the more restrictive fixed actuator. This session adds an explicit **nested-dynamics masking audit** and an independent **model-relative satisfiability** diagnostic.

The two models have identical state labels and initial state; every transition of the fixed model is allowed by the flexible model. Therefore any separating trace on the fixed model must also be allowed by the flexible model. Restricting physics can eliminate counterexamples without making a candidate natural-language interpretation correct.

Pinned exact expected classifications (pending current GitHub CI verification):
- Four changed offsets {1,2,4,6} in two directions = eight directional comparisons from **one** original instruction.
- Flexible model: seven separating directions.
- Fixed model: one separating direction.
- Six directions lose a witness under the restricted dynamics.
- Offset 1, when required to be true, is **unrealizable** on both models: state 0 cannot reach b at state 2 in one step. Its missing reverse witness is **not** counted as a masked disagreement.
- For offsets 2,4,6, both formulas can be satisfied on the fixed model; the absence of separation reflects their agreement on the model's unique allowed trajectory, **not universal semantic equivalence**.

This is a small classical model-checking demonstration. No new AI inference, real robot measurements, independent human interpretation labels, or population statistics were produced. No paper-level novelty is claimed.

## Scientific decision

The physics-model comparison exposes a methodological trap: **fewer discovered counterexamples under stricter physical assumptions may mean weaker error detection, not better AI reliability**. The next candidate contribution, if any, must quantify this on independent real model outputs and show an increment beyond established trace-sensitivity, vacuity and semantic-adjudication baselines. Without such evidence, pivot.
