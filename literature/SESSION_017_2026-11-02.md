## 3. Novelty audit: additional strong prior art

- [Wang et al., SCP-NL2TL, August 5, 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05439) already studies semantic-consistency signals, back-translation, conformal risk control and abstention for NL-to-temporal-logic translation. **A generic uncertainty/abstention method is not novel.**
- [Newcomb and Ochoa, August 6, 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06287) already evaluates 450 repeated NL-to-LTL candidates with manual semantic checks. **Repeated real-model outputs plus human checking are not novel.**
- [Quansah et al., VERIFY, ICLR 2026](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2026/hash/8b078da44614b6e249dc9e9474dc8d11-Abstract-Conference.html) already supplies a large contextual multi-domain LTL dataset with verified intermediate logic.
- [English et al., VLTL-Bench, 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.00877) already studies grounded trace-based formal translation evaluation.
- Classical model checking and vacuity predate all of these works.

**Scientific consequence:** We must not claim novelty for semantic checking, model checking, grounded traces, repeat consistency, abstention, or physical admissibility alone. A potentially useful *hypothesis* for later study is whether uncertainty over **multiple plausible physical dynamics** creates independently adjudicated failures missed by matched baselines. This is **not yet shown to be new**; the exact closest-method comparison and real-model experiment are still missing.
