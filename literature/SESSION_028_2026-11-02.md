## What the finding means

Our previous conditional 496/500 metadata-agreement figure is sensitive to whether proposition IDs that have identical *recorded* grounding entries really denote the same physical predicate. On nine real stored formula pairs, bounded counterexamples exist without that identity assumption. This establishes an **evaluation-assumption vulnerability**, not nine errors by an AI model, nine unsafe actions, or a new theorem.

The evidence does not identify which formula represents the original human intention. It also does not establish model provenance, independent source-family counts, physical-world validation, or a superiority result. This analysis was motivated by the 15 known mismatches and must remain labeled exploratory/post hoc.

## New closest prior art and originality restriction

Wang et al., [VerusEval](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26464), submitted October 19, 2026, already evaluate generated formal specifications beyond verifier acceptance and examine reference defects across 13,716 artifacts. Their abstract reports that 42.3% of verifier-accepted artifacts pass three semantic checks without a flag; this is their result in a different (Verus) domain, not ours. The broader evaluation motivation is therefore not novel. Classical bounded LTL model checking predates this work ([Biere et al., 1999](https://doi.org/10.1007/3-540-49059-0_14)). [VLTL-Bench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.00877), [ConformalNL2LTL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.21022) and [SCP-NL2TL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05439) remain strong relevant baselines.
