## Initial assessment
A generic combination of LLMs, world models, physical constraints, and formal verification is NOT a defensible novel contribution. Semantic drift and abstraction fidelity are also explicitly studied; merely identifying this issue is insufficient.

## Research hypothesis to challenge
Under controlled interventions, can a mathematically valid certificate remain misleading because its formal specification fails to preserve the intended physical semantics? Is there an algorithmically new and falsifiable method to quantify or detect this failure?

## Required next work
- Read full texts and related-work sections, not only abstracts.
- Identify exact closest methods, datasets, and theoretical guarantees.
- Define an operational semantic-fidelity metric with a ground-truth simulator.
- Specify negative controls, baselines, falsification conditions, and zero-cost cloud compute feasibility.
- Reject this direction if a materially equivalent contribution already exists.
