## 2. A consequential design correction: verifier must not see gold semantics

A dangerous but easy-to-miss mistake in our proposed research is using a reference formula or trace labeled `good_trace`/`bad_trace` as part of the method that decides whether a candidate formula is trustworthy. Those fields are evaluation oracles, not information available to a deployed verifier. A method given them could appear accurate simply because it was shown the answer.

[Protocol 022](../experiments/PROTOCOL_022_ORACLE_FIREWALL_AND_VLTL_ELIGIBILITY.md) was committed **before** code. New [`src/oracle_firewall.py`](../src/oracle_firewall.py) separates a strict six-field verifier-visible case from separately joined frozen decisions and post-hoc gold correctness. It rejects gold/trace fields in the verifier-facing case, duplicated source-family trial slots, missing/extra evaluation rows, non-Boolean decisions, and absent test source groups. It reports test-only case coverage, conditional accepted-case error (undefined under total abstention), unsafe acceptance, and source-family unsafe acceptance. The latter prevents treating repeated trials as independent observations. The [18 tests](../tests/test_oracle_firewall.py) use only constructed fixtures; **they are not frontier-model evaluation results**.

A synthetic identifiability warning shows that the same observable candidate and frozen decision can be scored correct or incorrect under two different externally assigned semantic labels. This is an elementary limitation, **not** a new impossibility theorem about language understanding: the true human meaning must be established independently, and an intelligent verifier may still infer meaning from the instruction.

A schema firewall is not a sandbox and cannot prove the absence of oracle leakage through developer code, training data, prompt construction, or evaluator-selected dynamics. Independent blind auditing is required before confirmatory evaluation.

## 3. Further prior-art pressure

- [nl2spec](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-37703-7_18) (Cosler et al., 2023) already uses LLMs, sub-translation feedback, and human interaction to resolve ambiguous temporal specifications.
- [SynthTL](https://github.com/dmmendo/synthTL) (Mendoza, Hahn, and Trippel, FMCAD 2024) already combines language-model decomposition, model checking, and human oracle guidance.
- [Gao et al., *Temporal Logic Trees for Model Checking and Control Synthesis of Uncertain Discrete-time Systems*](https://doi.org/10.1109/TAC.2021.3118335) (2022) already develops universal/existential temporal-logic reasoning for uncertain systems.
- VLTL-Bench (2025), SENTINEL (2025/2026), SCP-NL2TL (2026), and MANIGUARD (2026) already cover much of the language/logic/physics/selection intersection.

**Novelty is not established.** The narrow question remains whether independently justified uncertainty over physical assumptions provides incremental, deployable detection of unsafe formalizations at matched coverage, compared with strong existing methods, without test-gold leakage.
