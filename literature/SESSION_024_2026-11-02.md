## 3. A stronger novelty objection discovered

The literature search uncovered direct, substantial overlap:

- **SENTINEL**, https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.12985 , posted in October 2025 and reported as accepted to NeurIPS 2026, already verifies foundation-model safety across natural-language interpretation, plans, and physical trajectories with temporal logic and counterexamples. Its public code is at https://github.com/NU-IDEAS-Lab/SENTINEL .
- **MANIGUARD**, https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17386 (August 2026), already uses physics-grounded LTLf monitoring across 200 manipulation tasks and 1,000 ID/OOD scenarios.
- **SCP-NL2TL**, https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05439 (August 2026), already combines semantic verification, repeated-generation dispersion, and selective conformal abstention.
- **SecDev 2026 NL2LTL** already uses model checking for semantic consistency.

The [prior-art collision and candidate audit](PRIOR_ART_COLLISION_AND_DATA_CANDIDATE_2026-11-02.md) records why we cannot claim to be first to combine language, logic, and physics or to invent abstention, counterexamples, or semantic checking.

Our remaining **hypothesis**, not an established discovery, is that a diagnostic robust to uncertainty over independently justified physical dynamics might improve unsafe-acceptance risk beyond those baselines at genuinely matched coverage. An evaluator's gold human meaning must be kept hidden from the deployed diagnostic; using it to flag errors would invalidate the claim.

## 4. Decision and next falsification gate

The new 306-row source is potentially useful for a **development/input pilot**, not yet a confirmatory experiment: we do not know how many independent original requirements it represents, lack independently adjudicated meanings, and lack authenticated NL2FutureLTL model outputs. The next priority is to locate lawful per-item predictions and source-family provenance or a stronger benchmark that provides them. Compare any eventual candidate against SENTINEL-style semantic/physical checking and SCP-NL2TL-style selection using source-disjoint evaluation, frozen model budgets, and acceptance-overlap diagnostics.
