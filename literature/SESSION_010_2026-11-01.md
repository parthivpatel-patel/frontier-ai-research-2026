### What the three witness families prove (and do not prove)
1. **Fixed offset:** a at t=0 and b at t=3 forever absent otherwise satisfies `G(a -> X X X b)`, but not a changed instruction requiring b at t+1, t+2, t+4, or t+6.
2. **Relative offset:** a at t=0, b at t=1, c at t=2 satisfies `G((a & X b) -> X X c)`, but not a changed requirement that c occur **two steps after b** (t=3). This checks the time-reference anchor explicitly.
3. **Cardinality:** a true at every time, b and c false, satisfies `G(a | b | c)` but fails a changed requirement that **two** of a,b,c hold. This failure is robust to interpreting 'two' as exactly two or at least two. The original 'one of' phrase is ambiguous and **we do not certify its equivalence to the inherited formula**.

These are concrete counterexamples to inherited annotations under restricted grammar, not AI prediction errors, human-intent judgments, or physically realizable trajectories. The dataset creators explicitly said their inherited labels were unverified. We must credit their transparency and avoid alleging wrongdoing.
