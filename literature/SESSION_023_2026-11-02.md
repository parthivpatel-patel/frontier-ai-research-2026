## 1. A real methodological confound in Protocol 019

The previous paired endpoint counted a source as unsafe if *any* accepted generated formalization was incorrect. It correctly clustered repeats by original human requirement, but did not enforce an equal number of attempts per method. Thus a method generating more attempts could appear less safe even with the same underlying per-attempt behavior.

For a purely illustrative independent Bernoulli model with per-attempt unsafe probability `p`, the probability of **at least one** unsafe attempt in `k` attempts is `1 - (1-p)^k`. At p=0.1, this is **10.0% for k=1** and **40.951% for k=5**. This calculation does not estimate any real AI model's risk: attempts may be correlated, risks may vary by source, and the definition of "unsafe attempt" requires independent adjudication. The monotone dependence on the number of attempts is nevertheless a clear exposure confound.

The distinction is substantive: an *any-error among k* endpoint may be the right deployed-system risk if all k outputs are exposed, but is not a fair isolated verifier comparison when k differs between methods. This observation is classical repeated-sampling logic, not a new statistical result. Chen et al. (2021) already show the importance of multiple samples per prompt in code-model evaluation: https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.03374 .

## 2. Implemented fail-closed design gate

[Protocol 020](../experiments/PROTOCOL_020_BALANCED_EXPOSURE_GATE.md) was committed before executing its new cloud test suite. The [implementation](../src/balanced_exposure_gate.py) requires a predeclared complete Cartesian roster of **two methods × original source IDs × trial IDs**, with exactly one recorded outcome per slot. It rejects missing/duplicate/extra trials, unknown sources, invalid flags, incomplete correctness labels, and malformed rosters. Only after passing does it invoke the existing source-paired exact McNemar audit. It separately reports attempt-level accepted/unsafe counts and source-level any-error rates so their denominators cannot be confused.

A synthetic falsification test demonstrates the old gate's weakness: A has a safe first trial and an unsafe second trial; B has only a safe first trial. The old source-level audit reports A-only unsafe, while the new gate **rejects** the comparison as unbalanced rather than attributing the difference to verifier quality. A complete two-trial synthetic fixture for both methods passes. These are constructed fixtures, **not frontier-model observations**.

The [19 new regression tests](../tests/test_balanced_exposure_gate.py) cover the mathematical identity, exact trial-roster enforcement, missing adjudication, coverage collapse, source-versus-attempt denominators, and a nonzero method-swap sign check. ## 3. Scientific limitations and novelty gate

Equal **recorded** trial counts do not guarantee comparable prompts, model sampling distributions, temperature, token budgets, stop conditions, compute cost, or independence. The gate cannot authenticate model provenance or human-intended meaning. It also cannot ensure physical admissibility of an assumed dynamics model. The frozen k and any system-level fallback policies must be defined before looking at held-out correctness labels; sensitivity to k is secondary, not a license for post-hoc selection.

Closest prior art includes repeated-sampling evaluation (Chen et al., 2021), paired binary testing (McNemar/NIST: https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/software/dataplot/refman1/auxillar/mcnemar.htm), selective classification (Geifman and El-Yaniv, 2017), and the project's own Protocols 016–019. **Neither this gate nor the numerical example is a novel theorem.** The proposed paper still requires independently adjudicated human instructions, authentic model predictions, a source-disjoint test set, frozen strong baselines, independently checked physical assumptions, and matched-coverage evidence of improved unsafe acceptance. No such superiority evidence was produced in this session.

The earlier external dataset audit remains relevant: 1,790 augmented rows trace to only 36 original requirements, and mutation labels must not be treated as automatically correct. No new external labels were invented or accepted as ground truth.

## 4. Next work, feasibility, and cost

Priority is a lawful, source-disjoint evaluation population with independently checked natural-language meanings and actual model-generated formalizations. Predefine generation budget k and provenance for every method; then compare physics-assumption-aware diagnostics against established verification and calibrated abstention at matched coverage.
