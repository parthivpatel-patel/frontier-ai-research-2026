# Session 006 — Prior-art collision and semantic-oracle identifiability (2026-11-01)

**Decision:** The broad statistical-method direction is **NO-GO as an originality claim**. The specific combination of ambiguous ground truth and selective/conditional risk has strong existing literature. A narrow physics-grounded *evaluation protocol* remains a provisional candidate, not a novel result.

## Full-text / primary-source findings (date and source)

1. **Stutz et al., _Conformal prediction under ambiguous ground truth_**, TMLR **2023**. Google Research publication record: https://research.google/pubs/conformal-prediction-under-ambiguous-ground-truth/ . It already develops conformal prediction for intrinsically ambiguous expert labels and validates on synthetic and real datasets. **Eliminates** "calibration when ground truth is ambiguous" as a novel general idea.
2. **Bai and Jin, _Conformal Selective Prediction with General Risk Control_ (SCoRE)**, arXiv **25 March 2026**: https://arxiv.org/html/2603.24704v1 . The full HTML Section 2 explicitly distinguishes marginal deployment risk (MDR = E[L psi]) from selective deployment risk (SDR = E[sum L psi / max(1,sum psi)]), including semantic error in LLM outputs. Section 5 develops selective e-value control. **Eliminates** "first to control risk among accepted outputs" as a novel claim.
3. **Xu, Guo and Wei, _Selective Conformal Risk Control_**, arXiv **14 December 2025**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.12844 . It combines selection with risk control over accepted samples, with two finite-sample regimes. **Eliminates** generic selective conformal risk claims.
4. **Jin and Ren, _Confidence on the focal: conformal prediction with selection-conditional coverage_**, JRSS B, **15 April 2025**: https://academic.oup.com/jrsssb/article/87/4/1239/8113856 . It explicitly handles coverage conditional on being selected. **Eliminates** a generic selection-conditional coverage contribution.
5. **Wang et al., _SCP-NL2TL_**, arXiv **5 August 2026**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05439 . Calibrates acceptance/abstention of NL-to-temporal-logic specifications. **Eliminates** generic calibration of NL formalization.
6. **Feldman, Bates and Romano, _Conformal Prediction with Corrupted Labels: Uncertain Imputation and Robust Re-weighting_**, arXiv **7 May 2025**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.04733 . Addresses label uncertainty/corruption with robust conformal methods. **Eliminates** generic robust label-calibration as a contribution.

**Review caveat:** SCoRE's HTML formulation and Google Research's publication record were inspected directly. This is not an exhaustive full-proof or code reproduction audit of all six papers. Distinctions between marginal, selected and batch-selective risks must be maintained; do not assert these are mathematically identical.

## A deliberately simple impossibility witness

Let the observed instruction-to-formula candidate be `next_position <= 0.8`. The **supplied** reference set contains only `next_position <= 0.8`, so our exact toy auditor reports zero disagreement. Yet the intended reference could be `next_position <= 0.9` if the reference set was incomplete. At reachable next position 0.85, the candidate is false and the omitted intended reference is true. The same observed data are consistent with two different true interpretations and hence two different errors.

**Conclusion:** Without a completeness/validity assumption on the reference set, the true semantic error is not identifiable from that set alone. This is an elementary indistinguishability argument, **not a new theorem** and not proof that any published calibration method fails its own assumptions.

## Another metric trap: denominator

With 100 instructions, one accepted instruction, and that accepted instruction incorrect, the **observed** joint error fraction is 1/100 while the **observed** accepted-only error fraction is 1. This is elementary arithmetic, not a contradiction of SCP-NL2TL, SCoRE, or any population-level guarantee. SCoRE's SDR is an *expectation of a batch ratio*, not automatically identical to the conditional error probability or a realized batch ratio.

A potential research contribution is an independently adjudicated, physics-grounded evaluation of **semantic reference completeness under interventions**, measuring whether methods fail when the calibration oracle omits a plausible meaning. But this might already exist as a benchmark. It must be tested against prior benchmarks for formalization faithfulness, ambiguity and physically grounded counterexamples.
