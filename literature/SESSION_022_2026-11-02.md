## 1. Paired evaluation fixes a methodological weakness

Previous Protocol 018 produced marginal confidence bounds on conditional failure risk for fixed methods, but those bounds alone cannot establish superiority of one method over another. The new [pre-registered Protocol 019](../experiments/PROTOCOL_019_PAIRED_UNSAFE_ACCEPTANCE.md) defines a *source-paired* endpoint: for each original human requirement, did the verifier accept **any** incorrect formalization? It aggregates repeated outputs to one binary observation per method per original source.

The new [standard-library implementation](../src/paired_unsafe_acceptance.py) reports the full 2x2 paired table, the unsafe-acceptance rate difference on the **same eligible source population**, and the classical exact two-sided McNemar test on discordant source pairs. It also reports acceptance coverage, conditional failure rates and a caller-declared descriptive coverage gate. Critically, it explicitly refuses to interpret abstain-all as a reliability victory. The coverage gate is **not** a proof of equivalent population coverage; acceptance thresholds must be frozen on separate development sources for confirmatory matched-coverage inference.

The [15 synthetic regression tests](../tests/test_paired_unsafe_acceptance.py) check exact p-value boundaries, numerical underflow via log10 p, method-swap symmetry, any-error source aggregation, mismatched source populations, missing human labels, invalid flags and coverage collapse. For example, six discordant sources all favoring one method yield an exact two-sided p-value of **0.03125** under the null. This is **mathematical illustration only**, not observed model superiority or a new statistical result.

## 2. Newly discovered source-data integrity issue

The [external dataset audit](EXTERNAL_REQUIREMENTS_DATA_INTEGRITY_AUDIT_2026-11-02.md) examines Chiari and Mateis's *Datasets supporting automated requirement formalization from natural language to temporal logic*, [Zenodo DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23649257](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23649257), published **October 25, 2026**, and its [MIT-licensed GitHub source](https://github.com/michiari/requirements-datasets) pinned to commit `a38a73a351c07a3d17cb561098485cd4416c5375`.

The dataset has two relevant components, with important caveats:
- **1,790 augmented rows from 36 original NL/LTL requirements.** Rows are dependent perturbations, not 1,790 independent sources.
- **1,080 NL realizations from 360 STL formula seeds.** The source README explicitly states every `semantic_validation` is `not_checked`; these are not independently verified human semantic labels.

In a directly inspected small augmented CSV, an original instruction specifying **three steps later** is mutated to **two steps later** while the label `G(a -> X(X(X b)))` remains unchanged. The two instructions differ on an explicit infinite trace with `a` only at t=0 and `b` only at t=3. This is a concrete **potential gold-label mismatch if the augmented row is naively used as a correct translation**. The dataset authors intentionally supply robustness mutations; unchanged labels may be appropriate as perturbation references, not guaranteed correct translations. No model error or misconduct is inferred.

The permissive license and source-level grouping make the corpus useful for a **separately labeled robustness pilot**, but it cannot substitute for independently adjudicated physical meanings or authenticated frontier-model formalizations. We have not downloaded or redistributed the dataset in our repository.

## 3. Prior art and falsification

McNemar's exact paired test is established (NIST: https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/software/dataplot/refman1/auxillar/mcnemar.htm); selective classification and calibration are established (Geifman and El-Yaniv 2017; Learn then Test 2021/2025; SCP-NL2TL 2026). Grounded NL-to-LTL verification is already treated by VLTL-Bench (https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.00877), and geometric temporal translation with semantic checks by NL2SpaTiaL (https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.13670). Neither our statistical tooling nor spotting an intentional mutation is defensibly novel by itself.

The remaining hypothesis is **specific and untested**: independently validated physics-assumption-aware diagnostics might reduce *incorrect accepted formalizations* at genuinely matched coverage, beyond strong grounded-verification and calibrated-abstention baselines. Reject that claim if source-disjoint blinded data are unavailable, the dynamics assumptions exclude feasible trajectories, gains vanish at matched coverage, or the paired analysis fails pre-registered statistical criteria. No artificial model scores or human annotations were created.
