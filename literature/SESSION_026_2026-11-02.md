## New prior-art collision and alternative resource

[ConformalNL2LTL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.21022) (Wang et al., 2025) already combines LLM-based NL-to-LTL translation, conformal uncertainty quantification, and selective escalation. This is direct prior art for any claim about calibrated trust or asking for help. The [upstream implementation](https://github.com/kantaroslab/ConformalNL2LTL) carries a root Apache-2.0 license and provides `Dataset/final_calib.json` and `Dataset/max_scores.json` (directly inspected metadata and example schema). This offers a potentially better documented calibration baseline than an unlicensed dataset, but **upstream source rights and independent human semantic labels still require review**. See [dedicated collision audit](CONFORMALNL2LTL_PRIOR_ART_COLLISION_2026-11-02.md).

Other direct overlap: [SCP-NL2TL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05439) (2026), [SENTINEL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.12985) (2025), [VLTL-Bench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.00877) (2025), and classical uncertainty-aware temporal verification. **Novelty remains unestablished.**
