Across the **twelve formula strings** belonging to those six unsupported pairs, the fixed-vocabulary lexical census counted 16 occurrences of unknown-word tokens, 24 proposition occurrences, 52 parentheses, and known operator occurrences: and 10, finally 6, always 4, or 2. These are **token occurrences, not distinct operators, independent sources, or error counts**. Unknown token spellings were deliberately not printed, so we cannot yet identify the precise missing grammar operators. A narrow parser may reject valid LTL syntax; these six records are not automatically model errors.

**New methodological finding:** the entire six-pair coverage gap is consistent with a parser vocabulary limitation affecting both predicted and reference formulas, rather than an isolated prediction-only parsing problem. This is a descriptive diagnostic, not proof of the precise cause. Excluding these six would select on syntax and bias any claimed semantic accuracy. The original 9 counterexamples from Protocol 025 remain valid only within their stated assumptions; this session did not extend the semantics or recompute witnesses.

## Prior-art audit and scientific scope

A further close precedent is [NeuroNL2LTL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.22874), submitted May 20, 2026. Its authors already propose a neural-to-intermediate-representation-to-LTL pipeline with satisfiability checking and repair, and report evaluation across multiple safety-critical domains. Their claimed results have not been independently reproduced in this project. Together with [VLTL-Bench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.00877), [ConformalNL2LTL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.21022), [SCP-NL2TL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05439) and [VerusEval](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26464), this further rules out a broad novelty claim for merely adding formal checking to language-to-logic translation.

Our remaining potentially testable claim is narrow: **independently validated uncertainty about physical predicates/dynamics might improve detection of unsafe formal interpretations at matched coverage**. It has not been demonstrated. We have no independently adjudicated meanings, reliable source-family identities, or blinded matched-coverage model comparisons.
