# Literature audit — session 2 (2026-11-01)

## Decision

**Reject as a stand-alone novelty claim** the assertion that a world model can pass observational checks while being wrong for a rare, decision-critical intervention. Closely related work already demonstrates it. The project remains in Gate 1 (novelty and feasibility); no paper topic is locked.

## Additional closest prior work (primary paper records)

1. Javier Aguilar Martín, *When a Verified World Model Still Loses: Play-Adequacy vs Prediction-Accuracy in LLM-Synthesized Code World Models*, 15 July 2026, arXiv:2607.14169, https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14169. Reports high sampled transition accuracy with systematic downstream planning failures due to rare pivotal dynamics; develops a quantitative gate-miss expression. **High-overlap: decisive rejection of the generic verified-but-wrong model premise.**
2. Rongzhe Wei et al., *What Must a World Model Distinguish for Planning?*, 26 September 2026, arXiv:2609.33030, https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.33030. Defines mechanism/response/decision sufficiency and analyzes query-specific regret and planning interfaces. **High-overlap: decision-sensitive adequacy.**
3. Geigh Zollicoffer et al., *One Model, Two Physical Stories: Auditing Misalignment in Multi-Modal World Modeling*, 13 September 2026, arXiv:2609.14833, https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.14833. Compares generated physical predictions across modalities and against analytical dynamics with event/magnitude/timing contracts. **High-overlap: physical semantic misalignment auditing.**
4. Fabio Rovai, *CIVeX: Causal Intervention Verification for Language Agents*, 9 May 2026, arXiv:2605.09168, https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.09168. Studies identifiability, assumption-scoped causal certificates, and abstention in tool-using agents. **High-overlap: causal intervention certification.**
5. Atticus Geiger et al., *Causal Abstraction: A Theoretical Foundation for Mechanistic Interpretability*, JMLR 26(83), 2025, https://www.jmlr.org/papers/v26/23-0058.html. Formalizes causal abstraction and graded faithfulness. **High-overlap: abstraction-fidelity mathematics.**
6. Kecheng Liang et al., *PhysAlign: A Benchmark for Evidence-Grounded Role Alignment in Multimodal Physics Reasoning*, 27 September 2026, arXiv:2609.33319, https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.33319. Physics-diagram physical-role grounding failures. **Medium-overlap: semantics of physics-language evidence.**
7. Zheyu Chen et al., *Specification-Driven Generation and Evaluation of Discrete-Event World Models via the DEVS Formalism*, 4 March 2026, arXiv:2603.03784, https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.03784. LLM-generated executable world models checked against semantic/temporal trace constraints. **High-overlap: specification-driven model evaluation.**
8. Xi Zheng et al., *Assuring Learning-Enabled Cyber-Physical Systems with Foundation Models: A Neuro-Symbolic Agenda for Testing, Verification, and Runtime Assurance*, 29 October 2026, arXiv:2610.39547, https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.39547. Explicitly identifies specification engineering and abstraction fidelity as research challenges. **High-overlap: broad assurance architecture.**

These descriptions were verified against public abstracts/records; full-paper method-level comparison remains to be done. No claim of exhaustive coverage.

## Candidate direction to test, NOT claim as novel

**Budgeted, query-conditioned falsification of natural-language-to-physics abstractions.** Inputs: natural-language physical claim C, executable candidate model M, oracle simulator T, intervention set U, decision query q, and a fixed test budget B. Output: a counterexample or an abstention/limited assurance statement with explicit assumptions.

Question: Can a falsification strategy, under matched simulator-query budgets, identify semantic failures more efficiently than random interventions and uncertainty-only selection, while avoiding false certificates on physically equivalent models?

This direction overlaps the papers above. Before retaining it, compare algorithmic details and theoretical guarantees, including any query-conditioned active testing, and document a genuinely distinct hypothesis. A method is not novel merely because it combines existing components.
