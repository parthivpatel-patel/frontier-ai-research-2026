## 1. Alignment-free arithmetic on the previously audited NL2MTL outputs

[Protocol 015](../experiments/PROTOCOL_015_ALIGNMENT_FREE_CARDINALITY_BOUNDS.md) was committed before its implementation, but the five expression-count vectors were **already known** from Session 018. Therefore the exercise is a transparent **retrospective descriptive calculation**, not a blinded or prospective finding. [Implementation](../src/alignment_free_bounds.py) uses only those published count vectors, with [11 synthetic regression tests](../tests/test_alignment_free_bounds.py).

Across four original documents, each with five recorded runs, there are **40 unordered within-document run pairs** (10 per document), of which **30/40 have unequal numbers of extracted formula records**. If one hypothetically demands a one-to-one alignment of output records, the sum over all 40 pairs of the **minimum number of unmatched record occurrences** is **66**. Across the 16 adjacent-run pairs, the analogous sum is **26**. These sums repeatedly count some underlying output records across different comparisons; **66 is not 66 independent errors, missing requirements, or unique examples**.

| Original source | Sum of unavoidable unmatched occurrences, all 10 run pairs | Sum, 4 adjacent pairs |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Article 78 | 16 | 5 |
| Article 80 | 12 | 6 |
| CARA pump requirements | 22 | 8 |
| Harvesting process | 16 | 7 |
| **Total** | **66** | **26** |

For any pair with `n` and `m` expression records, the number of one-to-one matched pairs is at most `min(n,m)`, leaving at least `abs(n-m)` unmatched record occurrences. The maximum possible matched fraction relative to the larger output is `min(n,m)/max(n,m)`. The arithmetic mean of those 40 **upper bounds** is **70.580357%**. This is **not** observed semantic agreement, a confidence interval, or an accuracy score; true agreement could be zero for every pair. For two empty outputs the overlap fraction is deliberately undefined.

**Key identification limitation:** even when two runs contain equal numbers of expressions, cardinality alone cannot distinguish perfect semantic correspondence from no semantic correspondence. Count differences may reflect one-to-many or many-to-one decomposition, extraction granularity, or ambiguous text, rather than an erroneous formula. These elementary bounds are not novel mathematics.

## 2. Fail-closed source-disjoint evaluation infrastructure

[Protocol 016](../experiments/PROTOCOL_016_SOURCE_DISJOINT_EVALUATION_GATE.md) defines the independent unit as the original human-authored source and requires lawful model-output access, independent intent adjudication, and a minimum pilot set of 30 distinct original requirements before reporting confirmatory model-quality estimates. Thirty is a **practical minimum gate, not a power calculation or proof of adequacy**.

The new [source-disjoint planner](../src/source_disjoint_gate.py) uses seeded SHA-256 sorting and five group-level folds, rejects duplicate/invalid source IDs and datasets below the minimum, and prevents the same source appearing in train and test. Nine [synthetic-only regression tests](../tests/test_source_disjoint_gate.py) cover deterministic assignment, order independence, leakage prevention, duplicate rejection and the minimum gate. A synthetic set of 30 source IDs produced five six-source test folds with no leakage.

**The actual NL2MTL collection has only four independent original documents and therefore fails this gate.** The 20 output files and 98 extracted expressions cannot be promoted into 20 or 98 independent source requirements. No actual model was trained, evaluated, or compared in the new source-split exercise.

## 3. Novelty audit: more direct overlaps identified

The following literature further constrains any proposed novelty:

1. **Fushimi et al., *Structural-Ambiguity-Aware Translation from Natural Language to Signal Temporal Logic* (March 30, 2026)** already preserve multiple plausible interpretations and canonicalize formal candidates. https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.28426 . Therefore multi-interpretation handling is not novel by itself.
2. **Wang et al., *SCP-NL2TL* (August 5, 2026)** already combine repeated-translation semantic dispersion, back-translation and calibrated selective abstention. https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05439 . Therefore dispersion-based abstention alone is not novel.
3. **Amrollahi, Lopez and Barrett, *Faithful Autoformalization via Roundtrip Verification and Repair* (EMNLP Findings, October 2026)** already combine roundtrip translation, formal equivalence, diagnosis and repair. https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-emnlp.469/ . Therefore roundtrip equivalence checking alone is not novel.
4. **English et al., *VLTL-Bench* (2025)** already provide grounded NL-to-LTL verification across state spaces. https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.00877 .
5. **Godefroid (2013), may/must abstraction-based model checking**, already treats incomplete-system verification and falsification. https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/maymust-abstraction-based-software-model-checking-for-sound-verification-and-falsification/ .

**Provisional remaining research question, not an originality claim:** With *independently adjudicated* human intent and an explicitly uncertain physical model, can a combined assumption-aware diagnostic identify genuinely wrong AI-generated formalizations at a better matched false-accept/coverage tradeoff than all relevant verification, ambiguity-preserving and selective-prediction baselines? The contribution must survive source-disjoint evaluation, not merely a new arrangement of established components.
