# Cross-domain confirmatory decision rule and abstention guardrails (2026-11-20)

## Scope and evidence status
Prospective analysis specification only. No new model experiments, original statistical guarantee, or novelty claim. This note extends the frozen-threshold protocol without revising previously observed outcomes.

## Predeclared operational constants (provisional until frozen before held-out access)
Development target coverage c0 = 0.50. Coverage noninferiority margin delta_C = 0.05 absolute (five percentage points), selected as an operational tolerance, **not** a statistically or scientifically validated margin. Report sensitivity at 0.00 and 0.10; do not replace the primary margin after looking at outcomes. The primary comparator is the strongest conventional non-temporal verifier B selected using development data only. T is the temporal verifier; S is time-shuffled evidence; C is confidence-only. Freeze all preprocessing, scoring, costs, model versions, seeds, family IDs, and threshold tie-breaking before examining held-out domains.

## Primary per-domain success criterion
For each held-out domain d define coverage C_pd = sum_i A_pi / n_d and selective risk R_pd = sum_i A_pi(1-Y_i) / sum_i A_pi (undefined if denominator zero). Define coverage difference D_C = C_T - C_B and risk improvement D_R = R_B - R_T. A domain is provisionally successful only if (1) both denominators are positive, (2) a family-cluster confidence interval lower bound for D_C exceeds -0.05, (3) a family-cluster confidence interval lower bound for D_R exceeds 0, (4) T beats S under the same coverage/cost gates, and (5) no integrity or budget gate fails. These are conjunctive requirements; point estimates alone are insufficient. Report the number of independent source families and interval construction. If bootstrap resamples yield zero accepted answers, do not silently drop them: report their frequency and mark interval-based confirmation unavailable until an independently specified method handles them.

## Multiplicity and cross-domain claims
There are three held-out domains and two superiority contrasts (T vs B, T vs S), plus coverage gates. A global cross-domain success statement requires each domain to pass its predeclared gates, not an average gain. For any separate domain-level confirmatory claims, control familywise error across six superiority contrasts using Holm's step-down procedure on valid cluster-aware one-sided p-values; confidence intervals used for the conjunction should be calibrated consistently with the chosen testing family. If valid p-values cannot be obtained with available independent families, label all inferential results exploratory. Do not interpret a positive aggregate as evidence for every domain.

## Dataset and temporal feature validity
Use answer correctness adjudicated independently of verifier scores. A timestamp feature must correspond to information available at decision time. Distinguish repeated measurements of the same answer from independent model draws; log both provenance and clock ordering. A shuffled-control test must disrupt the temporal structure of interest while preserving marginal feature distribution and source-family constraints; if a valid shuffle is impossible, mark the temporal-specificity test inconclusive, not passed. Physics tasks must be scored against independent mathematical/physical answer criteria, not merely another model's preference.

## Budget accounting
At minimum record verifier model calls, input/output tokens, CPU seconds where measurable, wall time, and maximum memory; treat any missing dimension as unknown, not zero. Matched calls/tokens do not imply matched FLOPs, energy, or dollar cost. Predefine a hard resource cap and an abort-on-overrun rule for any future experiment. Never enable paid cloud services or local heavy compute; GitHub-hosted standard public-repo CI must be checked for eligibility and billing restrictions before execution.

## Novelty and publication gate
The contribution under test is the **specific empirical effect** of temporally ordered verifier evidence under frozen, source-family-disjoint, cross-domain transfer and strong non-temporal controls. General selective prediction, conformal risk control, confidence calibration, and ordinary temporal consistency are prior-art territory, not novel by assertion. A full-text nearest-neighbor comparison and actual held-out outcomes are required before claiming novelty. November 22 is a conditional checkpoint; absent experiments, publish only a transparently labeled protocol with disclosure of AI assistance as applicable.
